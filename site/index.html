    These recalls have a listing-style photo and have not been searched. One search costs one SerpApi search and takes a few seconds.

    How it works

    1. Take the seller's photo

      Each US recall notice carries photos of the product. We pick the one that looks like a store listing photo.

    2. Ask Google Lens for matches

      One search through SerpApi returns every page Lens matches to that photo, up to 400.

    3. Sort what comes back

      Rules read each address and title: store listing, news, social post or spam. Every listing keeps its link.

    Read the method

    Recall data: US Consumer Product Safety Commission. Image search: Google Lens through SerpApi.

    These are leads for review, not findings. A listing that matches a recall photo may be a different item, or may already be gone.
